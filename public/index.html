FAQ

Questions people ask

I'm a complete beginner. Is that OK?

Yes. Most members started from zero. Coaches scale every class to your level.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes, clean training shoes, a water bottle and a towel.

How do I book or cancel a class?

Book in the timetable above. Your confirmation has a link to cancel any time before class starts.

Do you have ladies-only classes?

Yes. Ladies Strength runs Saturday, Monday and Wednesday mornings with a female coach.

How do I pay?

At the front desk by cash, card or CliQ. There's no online payment.

Can I freeze my membership?

3-month memberships include one freeze week. Ask the front desk for longer breaks.
